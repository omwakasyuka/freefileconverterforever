Frequently asked questions

Is it really free forever?

Yes. There is no payment system anywhere on this site — no checkout, no card fields, no subscription. Convert and compress as much as you like.

Can I convert an image into a Word document?

Yes. Open the Image Converter tab, add one or more pictures, and choose Word (DOCX) from the “Convert to” list. Each image is placed full-page in a real .docx file that opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice and Pages. Selecting several pictures packs them all into one document.

Can I really convert PDF to Word?

Yes. The PDF's text is extracted and packaged into a genuine .docx file that opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice and Pages. Complex layouts and scanned PDFs are simplified — for scanned PDFs convert to PNG or JPG instead.

Can I really upload files up to 1 GB?

Yes. The limit is 1 GB (1024 MB) per file. Very large files take longer and use more memory because everything runs locally in your browser. For files over ~300 MB, close other tabs for best results.

How does compression work?

Images are resized (if you want) and re-encoded as JPEG at a quality level you choose. PDFs are re-rendered page by page. Multiple files are bundled into a single ZIP using maximum DEFLATE compression. Nothing is uploaded — it all happens on your device.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All conversion and compression happens inside your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your computer, which also makes it fast and private.

Will my converted file have a watermark?

Never. No watermarks, no branding burned into your output, no forced attribution.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed for anything. Only the administrator signs in, and that is just to view usage statistics.
